# Proof and evidence

- Use three outcomes only: proved, disproved, can’t tell yet.
- Absence of proof is not disproof.
- A verification you did not run counts as failed.
- Work you can’t prove yet ships as a draft PR flagged for human checks. Do not discard it silently.
- State what you ran, what you saw, and what remains unproven. Do not assert health without evidence.
